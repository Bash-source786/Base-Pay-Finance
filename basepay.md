# BasePay

BasePay is a free, interactive UK financial education simulator. Practise real-life money decisions with simulated money and see what happens when you change a decision.

## What you can practise

- Earn and budget: Understand a UK payslip and plan spending.
- Save and borrow: Explore savings, ISAs, pensions and credit.
- Invest and trade: Practise long-term investing and day trading with simulated money.
- Work through short lessons and scenarios, then try what you learnt in the simulator.

## How to start

Open [BasePay](https://basepay.org.uk/) and choose **Start free**. Choose Youth Mode for pocket money, saving and a Junior ISA, or Adult Mode for topics including pay, credit, pensions, investing and mortgages.

Guest progress stays in the current browser. Sign in with email to keep progress across devices and use three save files.

BasePay is an educational simulation. It does not connect to your bank, move real money or provide financial advice.
